3. Grammar (A Closer Look 2) • Ngữ pháp trọng tâm

The Present Simple Tense & Verbs of Liking / Disliking

Form: (+) S + V(s/es) / be (am/is/are) | (-) S + do/does not + V_inf | (?) Do/Does + S + V_inf? Usage: Expressing habitual actions, daily routines, general truths, and personal hobbies. Third-person singular spelling: add -s (reads), -es after -ch, -sh, -s, -x, -z, -o (watches, goes), consonant + y -> -ies (studies). Adverbs of frequency (always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never) stand BEFORE ordinary verbs and AFTER 'be'. Verbs of liking/disliking: like, love, enjoy, fancy, adore, hate, dislike, detest + V-ing. Expressions of preference: be keen on / be fond of / be interested in + N/V-ing; prefer A to B.
Ví dụ thực tế:
My brother collects coins and stamps. (Thói quen thường xuyên)
She doesn't like horse riding because she is afraid of falling.
Do you enjoy gardening in your free time? — Yes, I do.

3. Grammar (A Closer Look 2) • Ngữ pháp trọng tâm

Simple Sentences & Modal Verb 'Should / Shouldn't'

A simple sentence consists of one independent clause with a subject and a verb expressing a complete thought. Basic patterns: S + V (Birds sing) | S + V + O (We eat fresh vegetables) | S + V + Adverbial (He runs in the park every morning). Compound elements: A simple sentence can have compound subjects (Lan and Nam study) or compound verbs (He exercises and drinks water). Should / Shouldn't + V_inf: Used to give polite advice or recommendations (You should sleep early. You shouldn't skip meals).
Ví dụ thực tế:
Fresh fruit and vegetables provide essential vitamins. (Simple sentence: S + V + O)
You should drink at least two litres of water every day.
He shouldn't spend too much time playing computer games.

3. Grammar (A Closer Look 2) • Ngữ pháp trọng tâm

The Past Simple Tense (Regular & Irregular Verbs)

Usage: Actions or events completed at a specific time in the past. Time markers: yesterday, last week/month/year, two days ago, in 2022. Form with regular verbs: V-ed (clean -> cleaned, study -> studied, stop -> stopped). Form with irregular verbs: V2 (go -> went, have -> had, give -> gave, see -> saw, buy -> bought). Negative & Question: S + did not (didn't) + V_inf | Did + S + V_inf? To be in the past: was (I, he, she, it, singular) / were (you, we, they, plural).
Ví dụ thực tế:

They didn't organise the charity concert yesterday.
Did you donate your old warm clothes to poor children? — Yes, I did.

3. Grammar (A Closer Look 2) • Ngữ pháp trọng tâm

Nouns & Quantifiers (some, any, a lot of, lots of, much, many, How much/How many)

Countable nouns (eggs, apples, onions) take 'many', 'a few', 'How many + plural noun?'. Uncountable nouns (water, milk, beef, rice, flour, oil) take 'much', 'a little', 'How much + uncountable noun?'. 'some' is used in affirmative sentences and polite requests/offers (Would you like some tea?). 'any' is used in negative sentences and questions (Is there any milk left? There isn't any salt). 'a lot of / lots of' can be used with BOTH countable plural and uncountable nouns.
Ví dụ thực tế:
How many eggs do we need for the pancake? — We need three eggs.
How much olive oil is left in the bottle? — There is only a little.
We have got a lot of apples and some fresh milk.

3. Grammar (A Closer Look 2) • Ngữ pháp trọng tâm

Prepositions of Time (at, on, in) and Place

Time - at: specific clock times (at 7:30 AM), meal times (at lunchtime), fixed points (at noon, at night, at the weekend). Time - on: days of the week (on Monday), specific calendar dates (on 15th October), special days (on Christmas Day). Time - in: months (in July), seasons (in summer), years (in 2026), centuries (in the 21st century), parts of the day (in the morning). Place: in (inside enclosed space: in the classroom, in Hanoi), on (on surfaces: on the wall, on the 2nd floor), at (exact spot/institution: at school, at the bus stop). Relative positions: next to, between, opposite, behind, in front of, above, under.
Ví dụ thực tế:
