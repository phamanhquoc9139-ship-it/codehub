Grammar Breakdown (Ngữ Pháp Trọng Điểm)

Present Simple vs. Present Continuous
Present Simple: S + V(s/es) | S + do/does not + V | Do/Does + S + V? Present Continuous: S + am/is/are + V-ing | S + am/is/are not + V-ing
Present simple diễn tả thói quen, chân lí hiển nhiên hoặc sự sắp đặt ổn định. Present continuous diễn tả hành động đang diễn ra tại thời điểm nói hoặc tình huống tạm thời khác ngày thường.
Lưu ý: Không dùng thì tiếp diễn với các động từ chỉ trạng thái (stative verbs): know, like, love, understand, believe, own, belong.
My sister usually does the washing-up, but today she is studying for her exam.

In modern society, the traditional family structure where the father is the sole breadwinner and the mother manages all domestic chores has evolved remarkably. Ultimately, sharing chores should not be viewed as a burdensome obligation or a tedious penalty. Rather, it represents an essential daily expression of mutual empathy, respect, and solidarity, transforming any home into a truly supportive and harmonious sanctuary.

Climate change and rampant environmental pollution represent two of the most alarming challenges facing humanity in the twenty-first century. While international summits and government policies are essential, individual behavioral shifts play an indispensable role in safeguarding our biosphere. Adopting a 'green lifestyle' does not require radical, expensive sacrifices; rather, it begins with small, conscious modifications to our daily routines. One of the most effective measures is minimizing our carbon footprint through energy conservation. Simple actions such as turning off electrical appliances when leaving a room, unplugging idle chargers, and substituting energy-efficient LED bulbs for incandescent lighting collectively save enormous quantities of electricity. Furthermore, reconsidering everyday transportation by walking, cycling, or utilizing public transit significantly slashes greenhouse gas emissions. Another fundamental pillar of green living is practicing the 3Rs: Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle. Consumers can dramatically curb plastic waste by carrying reusable canvas tote bags, choosing refillable stainless steel water bottles, and rejecting single-use plastic straws and containers. By making mindful consumer choices and embracing zero-waste habits, each individual becomes an active guardian of the planet, ensuring a cleaner, healthier future for generations to come.
